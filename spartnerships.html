This is a monolithic website that I developed entirely on my own, handling every aspect from defining the project scope and initialization to building the frontend, backend, and database. The project was created for an organization in Limpopo with the goal of establishing a blog platform. Its purpose was to provide communities with insights into school performance, raise awareness, and foster the sharing of ideas.
